01 — How the Pathway Works

Every level, explained

Five levels, one progression. Here's exactly who can take each course, and what it takes to move to the next one.

01 · InstructorLevel 1

Learn to teach padel safely and effectively.

Build the foundations of the HELLO PADEL coaching methodology. Open to everyone, no matter your level — 100% online and self-paced. Arguably the most important course in the whole Pathway, and where every coach starts.

02 · BronzeLevel 2

Develop players with confidence.

Master technical coaching, lesson planning and player progression. Open to anyone, even before completing Instructor — but the final diploma and inclusion in the Coach Verification System require Instructor to be completed first. If you finish Bronze first, both diplomas are issued together.

Diploma requires Instructor completed
03 · SilverLevel 3

Develop players. Lead programmes. Manage clubs.

Go beyond coaching by learning how to organise coaching programmes, structure training systems, run events and tournaments, improve club operations, and create a professional coaching environment that helps both players and clubs grow. Builds directly on Bronze, and includes a mandatory practical exam with a Head Coach.

04 · GoldLevel 4

Lead coaches. Build academies. Create excellence.

Learn how to coach high-performance players while also developing coaching teams, mentoring instructors, managing academies, expanding operations across multiple locations, and creating a sustainable coaching business with the HELLO PADEL methodology. Completing Gold is what opens the door to Platinum.

05 · PlatinumLevel 5

Shape the future of padel coaching.

Become one of the highest-level educators within HELLO PADEL, capable of certifying coaches, leading international projects and helping expand the methodology around the world. Open exclusively to coaches who have completed Gold and are personally invited by Mauri Andrini and his team in Barcelona.

By invitation only

02 — The On-Court Event

On court, with our Head Coaches

An intensive, in-person practical course led by HELLO PADEL tutors. From the Bronze course onwards, the in-person part is a must to complete every stage: online, in-person and examination.

01

Methodology in Action

Our global coaching framework, applied in real sessions on court.

02

Exercises to Master

Dozens of hands-on drills you can use every day — combine them with the exercises from the videos you already have access to.

03

Tactical Coaching

Teach your players while they play the sport. Padel is more tactical than technical, and people want to play and have fun — the HELLO PADEL Method is the perfect mix to lead any level session.

04

Live Feedback & Assessment

Personal, on-court feedback that leads directly into the practical exam.
