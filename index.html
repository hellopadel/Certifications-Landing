01 — How the Pathway Works

Every level, explained

Five levels, one progression. Here's exactly who can take each course, and what it takes to move to the next one.

01 · InstructorEntry Level

Open to everyone, no matter your level

100% online and self-paced. Arguably the most important course in the whole Pathway: it's where we teach the real essence and methodology behind HELLO PADEL — the foundation everything else is built on. Every coach starts here.

02 · BronzeLevel 1

Open to anyone — but Instructor comes first

Anyone can take Bronze, even before completing Instructor. But to receive the final diploma and appear in the Coach Verification System, Instructor must be completed. If you finish Bronze first, both diplomas are issued together as soon as Instructor is done.

Diploma requires Instructor completed
03 · SilverLevel 2

Builds directly on Bronze

Deeper tactical methodology and communication. Includes a mandatory practical exam with a Head Coach.

04 · GoldLevel 3

Advanced, strategic coaching

Prepares coaches for advanced players, competition and performance planning. Completing Gold is what opens the door to Platinum.

05 · PlatinumLevel 4

Invitational only

Open exclusively to coaches who have completed Gold and are personally invited by Mauri Andrini and his team in Barcelona. The highest level of coaching in the HELLO PADEL Pathway.

By invitation only

Everything, in your language

02 — The On-Court Event

On court, with our Head Coaches

An intensive, in-person practical course led by HELLO PADEL tutors. From the Bronze course onwards, the in-person part is a must to complete every stage: online, in-person and examination.

01

Methodology in Action

Our global coaching framework, applied in real sessions on court.

02

Exercises to Master

Dozens of hands-on drills you can use every day — combine them with the exercises from the videos you already have access to.

03

Tactical Coaching

Teach your players while they play the sport. Padel is more tactical than technical, and people want to play and have fun — the HELLO PADEL Method is the perfect mix to lead any level session.

04

Live Feedback & Assessment

Personal, on-court feedback that leads directly into the practical exam.
